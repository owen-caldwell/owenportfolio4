Here we are! Time to decide a big grand project to work on for the rest of the semester. There are a few different directions I could go, and I've mapped out my thinking in the enormous canvas above.

## A Website

Every bone in my body is telling me to make a website. Thats what I'm in this program for after all! In a way this would be a great opportunity to take some extra time to get a portfolio together, or I could continue working on one of the many sites I've promised to artist friends of mine. Whatever the case, this is the kind of work that I'm most comfortable with, and it would probably be the easiest.

## Photography

Some part of me deep down wants to regress into my 1st love. Taking photos for a final project would be a great way to take a break from looking at screens all day. It's just a matter of choosing a topic and making a consistent effort to pick up a camera every day. I would also make prints to install for the exhibition.
