## The Exhibition

The kitchen at Harvestworks on Governors Island was a pretty unique space to install my project. I placed a CRT monitor where the sink would be, and ran cables to connect to my desktop linux computer under the sink. I set up my computer in a "kiosk mode" so that when it turned on it would automatically open the Night Shift script fullscreen with Firefox. I volunteered on Sunday morning right when Harvestworks opened.  I literally waited outside the locked door until Matthew showed up and let me in, and it didn't help that it rained that morning so it was pretty much just me and Matthew in the house setting up. After all the projects were turned on I said "Welp, anything else I can do?" Matthew shook his head and I took off. I wish I could've made it on Saturday to see people's reactions or feedback to my work.

## The Work

**I can't say I'm super proud of what I made here.** The way I see it, this project, and even my senior project which I'm more proud of, is only a start. But there's nothing bad about that, because I think if I were to continue down this road that the IDM program started me on, I'd be more prepared next time. I'm actually only just having this realization now, as before I've been pretty heavy-hearted by seeing this as a failure, when in reality its just the beginning of a new journey, possibly a life's work! With this project in particular I grappled with balancing concept and output, in other words, how important is meaning? Can I just make something meaningless but cool looking? The strongest work achieves a look that is both appealing and leaves the viewer wondering about it's meaning.
